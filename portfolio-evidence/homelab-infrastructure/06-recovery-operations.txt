HEXFORGE HOMELAB — RECOVERY AND STABILIZATION OPERATIONS
Historical event: July 2026
Evidence type: sanitized summary of existing internal recovery records

Historical operations timeline
1. Assessed current container and service state before making cleanup choices.
2. Recovered and validated monitoring, administration, file-management, and
   collaboration services, including Uptime Kuma, Portainer, File Browser,
   Nextcloud, and OnlyOffice.
3. Confirmed the core HexForge application, database, model, assistant,
   heightmap, and surface services.
4. Classified stopped and legacy services before considering removal.
5. Identified persistent data that required protection and preservation.
6. Completed a critical-service backup set before destructive maintenance.
7. Verified that the existing backup artifacts matched their SHA-256 manifest.
8. Recorded a stabilization decision: no destructive cleanup during the pass.

Operational decision pattern
ASSESS -> STABILIZE -> CLASSIFY -> PROTECT DATA -> VERIFY -> CONSIDER CLEANUP

What this demonstrates
- Dependency-aware service recovery and validation
- Data-protection decisions before cleanup
- Classification of ambiguous or legacy resources instead of blind deletion
- Use of service state, health checks, and documented verification
- A cautious, reviewable recovery workflow

Sanitization
This artifact paraphrases internal records. Raw recovery files, inspection
JSON, addresses, topology, protected volume names, unrelated project names,
management endpoints, credentials, and personal data are omitted.

Limitations
This is historical evidence, not current runtime state. It does not establish
that every backup has been restored, that every service was continuously
available, or that a formal disaster-recovery drill was completed. The backup
workflow was documented as manual at the time.
